# 知识库 · 生图提示词

给其他生图 AI 用。知识库是浅色「档案」风格：纸色底、墨黑细线、等高线、点阵、小号工业标签。参考明日方舟终末地官网的浅色版面，以及明日方舟官网干员档案页的人物摆放。只借鉴风格，不要画任何明日方舟的角色、标志或文字。

## 页面配色

画面尽量落在这几种颜色里，页面会自然融进去。

| 用途 | 颜色 |
|---|---|
| 纸色底 | `#EEECE5` |
| 亮面 | `#F7F6F1` |
| 墨黑 | `#121412` |
| 次级灰 | `#757A73` |
| 信号绿（少量点缀） | `#13A065` |
| 优品金（只在枪上出现） | `#B48A2C` |

## 一、知识库首页横幅（最需要）

- **文件名**：`hero.jpg`，放到 `wiki/preview/assets/`。放进去刷新页面就会自动替换现在的线稿占位。
- **尺寸**：2400 × 960，横向，约 5 : 2。
- **构图**：主体放在画面右侧六成。左侧四成要留出干净的浅色空白，页面会在那里压上「SHUXIN的资料收集」大标题和数据。
- **手机端**会裁成更窄的比例，主体最好集中在右侧中部，不要贴边。

### 方案 A：纯枪械（稳妥）

```text
light industrial archive illustration, off-white paper background #EEECE5,
two or three tactical assault rifles with ornate luxury weapon skins
(gemstone inlays, gold trim, holographic paint, glowing light strips),
floating in an exploded technical-drawing layout on the right side of the frame,
thin black ink contour lines, topographic map lines, dot-matrix grid, small
monospace labels and index numbers, measurement ticks, barcode strips,
minimal clean composition, generous empty space on the left 40 percent,
muted palette with a few accents of signal green #13A065 and gold #B48A2C,
crisp vector-like linework mixed with high-detail product render,
editorial layout, flat lighting, high resolution
```

### 方案 B：娘化砖皮（2026-10-21 决定暂不采用，保留备查）

参考你放的干员档案截图：一位角色站在右侧，背后是放大的半透明人物剪影和大号英文字。

```text
anime style character illustration, a young female tactical operator
personifying an ornate luxury rifle skin, holding a decorated assault rifle
with gemstone inlays and gold trim, standing on the right side of the frame,
three-quarter view, confident pose, modern tactical outfit in black, white
and muted green, light industrial archive background, off-white paper
#EEECE5, faint enlarged grayscale silhouette of the same character behind
her, thin black ink contour lines, topographic lines, dot-matrix pattern,
small monospace labels, clean empty space on the left 40 percent,
accents of signal green #13A065 and gold #B48A2C, high detail, crisp
lineart, soft flat lighting, high resolution
```

### 两个方案都不要

```text
dark background, neon cyberpunk city, heavy bloom, lens flare, blood, gore,
text, letters, watermark, logo, game UI, Arknights characters, Arknights logo,
cluttered composition, subject covering the left side, photo-realistic people
```

## 二、枪种方格插画（可选）

首页的 14 个黑色方格现在用的是图鉴封面截图。如果想统一画风，可以每把枪画一张。

- **文件名**：`assets/tiles/枪名.jpg`，例如 `assets/tiles/K416.jpg`。
- **尺寸**：1024 × 1024，正方形。
- **构图**：主体居中偏上，下方三成要暗，页面会在那里压白色枪名。
- 放好后告诉我，我把方格改成优先读这里的图。

```text
square 1:1 illustration, dark charcoal background #161917, a single
{WEAPON} rifle with the {SKIN} luxury skin, centered slightly above middle,
dramatic side lighting, subtle rim light in signal green, fine ink
technical lines and a faint dot grid in the background, bottom third
fades to near black, product render quality, no text, no logo
```

把 `{WEAPON}` 和 `{SKIN}` 换成下表。皮肤名取自图鉴封面。

| 枪名 | 封面皮肤 |
|---|---|
| ASVAL | 悬赏令 |
| AUG | 气象感应 |
| AKM | 群星 |
| K416 | 命运王牌 |
| KC17 | 造物纪元 |
| M250 | 电玩高手S2 |
| M4A1 | 棱镜攻势 |
| MK4 | 疾光魅影 |
| M7 | 棱镜攻势S2 |
| MP7 | 电玩高手S2 |
| QBZ95 | 王牌之剑 |
| SCARH | 电玩高手 |
| Vector | 美杜莎 |
| 腾龙 | 气象感应 |

生图 AI 不认识游戏里的皮肤名，所以最好同时把图鉴里的封面图当参考图喂进去，让它只改画风。

## 三、全站纸纹（可选）

现在的等高线是代码画的，已经能用。如果想要更有质感的纸面，可以出一张无缝底纹。

- **文件名**：`assets/paper.jpg`，2048 × 2048，必须能无缝平铺。

```text
seamless tileable texture, off-white archival paper #EEECE5, extremely
subtle fiber grain, very faint topographic contour lines in light gray,
almost invisible, flat even lighting, no vignette, no text
```
